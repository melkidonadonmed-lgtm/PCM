## 1. Overview & Active Architecture
- **Purpose:** 100% client-side clinical, outpatient, and inpatient SPA for Brazilian medical practice: digital prescriptions, weight-gated pediatric calculator (`"sem peso, sem dose"`), exam orders, CFM certificates/referrals, 9 official SUS vector sheets, hospital ward/bed management (`MOD-03`), and 114 CDSS clinical calculators on unified A4 print sheets.
- **Stack & Runtime:** TypeScript 5.8+ / Node LTS + React 19 + Vite 6.4+ + Vitest 5.1 + Tailwind CSS v4.3.5 + Dexie.js 4.4.7 + `html2canvas-pro` + `jspdf` + Firebase 12 (optional). Deployed via Cloud Run (`prescmed` in `southamerica-east1`, `https://precmed.web.app`).
- **Active Modules:**
  - `src/components/prescription/` & `src/components/documento/` (`MOD-01` — `Alt+1..6`): Simple, antimicrobial (2-copy RDC 471/2021), and controlled (2-copy Portaria 344/98) prescriptions, pediatric calculator, exams (14 routines), certificates/referrals, A4 Editor (Tiptap 3), and 9 SUS vector forms (`APAC`, `LME`, `SISREG`, `GAL/LACEN`, `SISCAN`, `TRM-TB` on `.sus-grid` 12-col).
  - `src/components/hospital/` & `src/utils/hospital/` (`MOD-03` — `Alt+7`): Bed census (`EF/LT`), structured evolution, non-hallucinating multimodal ingestion, longitudinal vitals (`tabular-nums`), *Conduct x Evolution* 6-axis cross-check, and Science Copilot.
  - `src/components/scores/` & `src/utils/scores/` (`Scoreboard CDSS` — `Alt+8`): 114 calculators across 9 lazy-loaded block chunks, `EPARADA` (ACLS/PALS + 5H/5T), INCA/MS oncology screening, continuous infusion (`BIC`), physiological radar, and bed bridge (`HospitalBedScoreEntry`).

## 2. Critical Technical Decisions (Do Not Violate)
- **Single Source of Print Truth (`FolhaA4.tsx` + `DocumentoClinico.tsx`):** On-screen preview, `window.print()`, and PDF export (`exportarFolhaPdf`) render the exact same A4 component (`#FFFFFF`, zero internal `sm:`/`md:`/`dark:` utilities).
- **YMYL Clinical Invariants:** `"Sem peso, sem dose"`; decimal mg formatting with comma and **zero thousands separator** (`"1000 mg"`); zero fabricated clinical values; ICD-10 on certificates strictly gated on `authorizedByPatient === true` (Res. CFM 1.658/2002); local dates via `dataLocalISO`.
- **Design System (`DESIGN.md` — `design-md v8.0.0` + `shredded-shape` WCAG 2.2 AA/AAA):** Elevated Clinical Matte surfaces (`#EFF3F8` canvas, `#FFFFFF` projected cards & controls with `#8594A6` `3.10:1` SC 1.4.11 borders Light; `#141C27` canvas, `#243042` cards with zenithal specular rim-light, `#2C3B50` projected inputs with `#73869F` `3.05:1` borders, `#202B3C` modals, `#C5DCF8` AAA `--ink-accent` Dark), lean headers (`text-sm font-semibold` + `<Icon size={18} />`), compact rhythm (`p-3.5 sm:p-4`, `gap-3`), `.touch-target` (`>= 44px` fine / `48px` coarse), and offline WOFF2 `material-symbols` subset (`39.5 KB`, PWA precache `< 5 MB`).
